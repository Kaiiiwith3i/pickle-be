# Pickle C-LB Privacy Policy

*Effective: [set on publication] · Contact: [client email]*

Pickle C-LB ("the App") is operated for the pickleball community of Calamba
and Los Baños, Laguna. We comply with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic
Act No. 10173)** and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

## What we collect

- **Profile data you provide**: display name, skill tier and optional
  self-rating, weekly availability windows, play preferences, and your chosen
  **home area** (a barangay-level location — we never collect your exact
  address, and GPS is used only if you explicitly enable it for map centering).
- **Activity data**: OpenPlay sessions you join or host, court bookings, posts
  and likes, and match participation records.
- **Device data**: a push notification token, if you allow notifications.

## How we use it

- Matchmaking: your tier, availability, and area compute compatibility scores
  and distances. **Other players never see your coordinates** — only distances,
  shared time slots, and your area label.
- Court discovery and booking; OpenPlay notifications to eligible players near
  a session's court.
- Anonymized, aggregated participation records improve the matchmaking model.
  This use is disclosed at onboarding and covered by your **opt-in** consent.

## Your rights (RA 10173)

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or
object to processing at any time. In-app: Profile → matchmaking toggle
(withdraws matchmaking visibility immediately) and Profile → Reset (deletes
your profile). For full data export or erasure requests, contact the operator;
we respond within 15 working days.

## Storage and sharing

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) in the `asia-southeast1` region,
protected by authentication and security rules. We do not sell or share
personal data with third parties. Push notifications are delivered via Expo's
notification service, which receives only the device token and message.

## Retention

Profiles persist until you delete them. Booking and session records are
retained for 12 months for community statistics, then anonymized.

## Children

The App is intended for users 13 and older; players under 18 should register
with guardian consent.
